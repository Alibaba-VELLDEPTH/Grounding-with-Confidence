A NOTE ON THESE CASES

These selected successful examples use an earlier RL variant, not the final model behind the quantitative results above.

Case thresholds are historical test-oracle settings. They illustrate mechanisms, not threshold transfer.

WHAT CONFIDENCE CANNOT DO

Selection is not a substitute
for finding the event.

A selector cannot recover a missing candidate or reverse an NMS suppression. Most of the full-system F1 gain comes from NMS; the additional confidence gain after NMS is not statistically established.

Read the evaluation boundaries +

The final model's incremental tF1@0.5 gain after NMS is +0.32 points, with a 95% source-video bootstrap interval of [−0.14, 0.76]. Manuscript checkpoint and test-selected threshold results are disclosed retrospective diagnostics. Scores do not establish probability calibration; tested precision targets did not transfer reliably. Synthetic rejection results do not establish general open-world rejection.
